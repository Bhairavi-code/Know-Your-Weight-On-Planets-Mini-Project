# 🪐 Know Your Weight on Planets

**Know Your Weight on Planets** is a visually immersive web app that calculates your weight across different celestial bodies in our solar system. With a space-themed video background and planet illustrations, it offers a fun and educational experience that blends science with design.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🌌 **Space-Themed UI**  
  A looping space travel video sets the mood, complete with ambient sound for a cosmic feel.

- ⚖️ **Weight Calculator**  
  Enter your Earth weight and instantly see how much you'd weigh on:
  - Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto

- 🖼️ **Planet Visuals**  
  Each planet is represented with a custom `.png` image and your calculated weight displayed below.

- 🔇 **Audio Control**  
  Toggle background audio with a simple mute/unmute button.

---
